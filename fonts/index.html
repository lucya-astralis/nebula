The logo face drawn from the mark

Nebula Wordmark is set beside the eagle, so it is drawn from the eagle. The mark has four angles and nothing else, and the face uses only those: 2:1, the tail diamond and the steep wing edges, for the W, M and 7 and for every horizontal end, cut like a feather tip; 4:3, the outer wing tips, for the A and V; 1:2, the long top edge of a wing, for the K's arms; 1:1, the cuts across the feather ends, for every corner that would have been round. A point is the tail itself, a rhombus twice as tall as it is wide. The stance is still Ethnocentric's, heavy and set tight with the A on its foot, only narrower and without a round; wedge pairs are kerned until they sit as close as two stems, so the A slides under the Y.

  • 6.4

  • Y

  • 9.0
    Double · M W — W is two wedges sharing a leg; M is one wedge hung between two stems.

        Nebula Icons

        The fourth face: line icons on an 8 × 8 grid, drawn by the same stroker, so an icon is one more glyph of the label beside it. Names say what an icon is for. Hover one for its class.

          How it is drawn 6

          • 01
            One stroker, four faces. Every glyph is a few strokes on a grid: Mono and Display on 4 × 6, the Wordmark on a square grid six units to the cap, the icons on 8 × 8. Same pen, same cut, so the logo, the furniture and the icons cannot drift apart.
          • 02
            The eagle's angles. The mark has four: 2:1, 4:3, 1:2 and 1:1. The Wordmark uses only those, its horizontal ends are cut like feather tips, and its points are the tail diamond.
          • 03
            Cut, never rounded. A bowl is a box with its corners cut at 45°. That is rule 2 carried into the letters: the language has no radius, so its type has none either.
          • 04
            The stroke is inset. The grid is mapped inside the stroke, so a stem on the baseline ends on the baseline at every weight; only the counters close as the weight rises. A Wordmark wedge is trimmed flat on the line, never past it.
          • 05
            Tabular by construction. Every Mono glyph is 600 units wide, so counts, sizes and EXIF rows line up without font-variant-numeric. The zero is slashed, the one has a foot, the l turns right: 0O 1lI never collide.
          • 06
            Static, small, no local(). Seven files, 2.4–4.3 KB each and about 26 KB together, all built by assets/fonts/build.py. Weight ranges as the language already uses: 100 450, 451 550, 551 650, 651 900.
